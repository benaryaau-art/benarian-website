01 · COMMERCIAL FOUNDATION

Begin with a clear reason to choose the hotel.

Define the property’s most valuable guest problems, distinctive experiences and proof points. Translate them into a positioning statement that sales, digital content, distribution and hotel operations can all deliver consistently.

Commercial strategy

  • Market and competitor set
  • Target segments and need periods
  • Value proposition by segment
  • Rate and channel principles
  • Annual revenue and profit goals

Operating alignment

  • Product and room readiness
  • Service promises sales may make
  • Capacity and event constraints
  • Lead and booking handovers
  • Guest feedback returned to commercial teams

03 · SALES PROCESS

Make every lead visible, owned and time-bound.

  1. Capture every lead in the CRM with source and time received.
  2. Qualify dates, budget, decision process, space and total value.
  3. Check inventory, displacement and operational feasibility.
  4. Build a tailored proposal with clear inclusions and validity.
  5. Record follow-up dates and next action before closing the task.
  6. Negotiate within approved authority and document concessions.
  7. Contract, collect deposits and confirm credit requirements.
  8. Complete a structured handover to operations and billing.
Lead standard: acknowledge quickly, qualify before discounting, and measure conversion by salesperson, segment, lead source, value and loss reason.

04 · MARKETING ENGINE

Build demand through a planned customer journey.

Brand & content

Use consistent photography, proof, tone and offers across the website, social media, partner listings and sales materials. Content must answer who the hotel is for, why it is different and why to book now.

Direct demand

Strengthen search visibility, landing pages, booking-engine usability, rate clarity, remarketing and email journeys. Track the path from impression to profitable booking.

Campaign planning

Every campaign needs a segment, objective, need period, offer, channel, budget, landing page, tracking code and post-campaign review.

CRM & retention

Capture consent and preferences, segment the database, automate pre-arrival and post-stay messages, and create relevant reasons to return directly.

05 · DISTRIBUTION

Judge channels by net contribution.

Channel decisions should account for commission, transaction cost, loyalty cost, cancellation behaviour, payment terms, visibility value and displacement. Keep content, room types, policies and availability accurate across all channels.

Net ADR = room revenue − commission − channel cost − offer cost
Compare like-for-like bookings and include cancellation and payment risk.

Direct channel controls

  • Mobile booking journey and speed
  • Clear inclusions and cancellation terms
  • Rate confidence and member value
  • Accurate analytics and conversion tracking

Third-party controls

  • Rate and inventory accuracy
  • Content and image quality
  • Commission and promotional cost
  • Market reach and incremental demand

Run a regular commercial meeting with a shared view of pickup, pace, forecast, need dates, group pipeline, campaigns, channel production and competitor activity. Record decisions, owners and expected impact.

07 · KPI SCORECARD

Measure volume, value, cost and conversion.

Revenue performance

Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, total revenue, TRevPAR, segment mix, room-type mix and market penetration.

Sales productivity

Qualified pipeline, response time, proposal conversion, account production, sales activity quality and lost-business reasons.

Marketing efficiency

Website conversion, cost per acquisition, return on ad spend, direct revenue, email revenue, engagement and assisted conversion.

Distribution health

Direct share, channel cost, net ADR, parity accuracy, cancellation, lead time and booking-window mix.

Conversion rate = confirmed business ÷ qualified opportunities

ROAS = tracked campaign revenue ÷ advertising spend

08 · 90-DAY PLAYBOOK

Move from diagnosis to disciplined execution.

Days 1–30 · Diagnose

  • Audit positioning, channels and content
  • Clean CRM pipeline and account records
  • Review segment profitability and need dates
  • Confirm KPIs and reporting ownership

Days 31–60 · Activate

  • Launch targeted account and campaign plans
  • Improve high-intent landing pages
  • Set weekly commercial rhythm
  • Retrain lead handling and proposal standards

Days 61–90 · Optimise

  • Shift budget toward profitable sources
  • Close content and parity gaps
  • Review conversion and lost business
  • Build the next rolling 90-day plan

Owner / GM view

Report the commercial story in plain language: what changed, why it changed, the profit impact, the next decision and the accountable owner.
