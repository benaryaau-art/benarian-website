BENARIAN Luxury Travel & Hospitality

BENARIAN is an Australian luxury travel and hospitality brand created to make exceptional hotels, resorts and personalised travel experiences easier to discover. Founded by hotel executive Ben Tafreshi, the brand combines more than two decades of international hospitality experience with carefully selected destinations, trusted booking partners and personal travel support.

What BENARIAN Does

BENARIAN showcases luxury hotels, boutique resorts, flights, wellness retreats, restaurants, cultural experiences and private journeys. Our platform helps travellers explore selected destinations, compare inspiring places to stay and connect with trusted travel partners for current rates and availability.

Our initial destination focus includes Bali, Phuket, the Maldives, Dubai and selected Persian travel experiences, with further international destinations added as the collection grows.

Our Difference

BENARIAN is built around genuine hotel and resort knowledge rather than anonymous listings alone. We focus on the quality of the stay, location, service, design, guest experience and the details that make a journey memorable.

Travellers can also contact BENARIAN directly for personalised recommendations, itinerary guidance and luxury travel assistance.

A Hospitality-Led Travel Brand

Ben Tafreshi has worked across luxury hotels, resorts, food and beverage, rooms division, property operations and guest experience in Australia and internationally. BENARIAN brings that operational hospitality perspective into travel discovery, helping guests understand not only where to stay, but why a particular hotel, resort or destination may suit their journey.
