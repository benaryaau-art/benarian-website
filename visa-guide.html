Planning information for Iranian travellers visiting Bali and Phuket.

Before you travel

Visa rules depend on nationality, destination, passport validity and travel purpose. Confirm all requirements with the relevant immigration authority before payment or departure.

01

Check entry rules

Confirm visa type, permitted stay, passport validity and onward-ticket requirements.

02

Prepare documents

Keep accommodation confirmations, return flights, insurance and financial evidence ready.

03

Confirm officially

Requirements and service charges can change. Verify the final amount before payment.
